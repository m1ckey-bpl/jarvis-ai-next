# GET THE IPA — PROJECT JARVIS

This guide describes how to download, sign, and install the **JarvisApp** unsigned IPA on your iPhone.

---

## 1. Download the Artifact

The unsigned IPA is generated by the GitHub Actions CI pipeline and published directly to GitHub:

1. Visit **[Releases in jarvis-ai-next](https://github.com/m1ckey-bpl/jarvis-ai-next/releases)**
2. Download `JarvisApp-1.0.0-unsigned.ipa` and the corresponding `.sha256` checksum.
3. (Alternatively) Download the `JarvisApp-1.0.0-unsigned-ipa` artifact from the latest successful **[iOS Build Workflow Run](https://github.com/m1ckey-bpl/jarvis-ai-next/actions/workflows/ios.yml)**.

---

## 2. Sideloading Options (Zero-Cost)

Because this IPA is unsigned (`CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO`), it must be signed with your free personal Apple ID before iOS will execute it.

### Method A: Sideloadly (Recommended — Windows & macOS)
1. Download and install [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io/).
2. Connect your iPhone via USB (or ensure Wi-Fi sync is enabled).
3. Drag `JarvisApp-1.0.0-unsigned.ipa` into Sideloadly.
4. Enter your Apple ID email.
5. Click **Start** to sign and install the app onto your iPhone.
6. On your iPhone: Go to **Settings → General → VPN & Device Management**, tap your Apple ID, and select **Trust**.
7. Open **JARVIS**!

### Method B: AltStore
1. Install [AltServer](https://altstore.io/) on your PC/Mac.
2. Install AltStore onto your iPhone.
3. Open AltStore on your iPhone, go to **My Apps**, tap **+**, and select `JarvisApp-1.0.0-unsigned.ipa`.
4. AltStore will sign and install the app.

---

## 3. First Launch Setup

1. Open **JARVIS**.
2. Complete the initial onboarding and grant **Microphone**, **Speech Recognition**, and **Notifications** permissions when prompted.
3. Go to **Settings → AI Provider**:
   - Select **Google Gemini** (or **Groq**).
   - Paste your free API key.
   - Tap **Save & Activate Provider**.
   - Tap **Test Connection** to confirm connectivity.
4. Return to the Home screen and converse naturally with JARVIS!
