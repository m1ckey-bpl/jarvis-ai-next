# DESIGN SYSTEM — JARVIS LIQUID GLASS

## Principles
1. **Liquid Glass & Spatial Layering:** Deep translucent materials with subtle borders (`Color.white.opacity(0.12)`) and soft radial glows.
2. **Restrained Color Palette:**
   - Background: Deep graphite & pure obsidian (`#0A0A0F`)
   - Surface: Elevated slate (`#14171F`)
   - Accent: High-luminescence cyan (`#00D9FF`)
   - State Colors: Thinking (`#FF991A`), Success (`#33E680`), Destructive (`#FF4D4D`)
3. **Ergonomic Touch Targets:**
   - Minimum 44pt hit targets across all buttons and tabs.
   - Sibling layout architecture ensuring no control is obscured by floating bars or safe areas.
4. **Digital Visage:**
   - Animated geometric cranium with glowing optic eyes that blink naturally.
   - 7-segment aperture mouth reacting in real-time to speech synthesis visemes.
